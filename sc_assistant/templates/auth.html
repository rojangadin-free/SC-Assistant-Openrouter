Student Data Access Agreement

By creating an account, you authorize the Samar College AI Assistant to securely access, process, and use your student information for personalized academic assistance.

This access includes:

  • Current and historical academic grades
  • Class schedules, enrolled subjects, and room assignments
  • Tuition assessments, financial balances, and payment history

Your information is processed securely and is used solely within the Samar College AI Assistant. We do not sell or share your data with unauthorized third parties.
