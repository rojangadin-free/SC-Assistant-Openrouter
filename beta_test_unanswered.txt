Yes — based on the 2024 Samar College Academic Awards guidelines, transferring does affect eligibility in a specific way:

📌 Key Rule for Transferees

A Cum Laude candidate must have no rating remarks of 5.0 (FAILED), Incomplete (INC.), or Dropped (DR.) and no grade below 2.50 in any subject [1].
